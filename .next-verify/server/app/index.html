Slow Fashion Atelier

A Quiet Meditation in Every Bead

At Nomiki, jewelry is not manufactured; it is woven. Under the slow-fashion ethos, each piece requires hours of meticulous hand-beading. We use premium Japanese Miyuki delica seed beads, natural freshwater baroque pearls, and semi-precious quartz stones, bound with durable silk thread and finished with 14k gold-filled metals that resist tarnishing.

Every bead is a stitch in time, a slow meditation of structure and color. We do not chase fast trends; we capture organic elegance in micro-art.
— Nomiki, Head Artisan
